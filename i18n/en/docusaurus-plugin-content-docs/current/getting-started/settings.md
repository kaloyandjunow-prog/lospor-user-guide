# Your settings

Open the **⚙** icon at the top right (**Account & Settings**), then
**Settings**. On the phone, the gear in the header. Your choices are saved
with your account and follow you to every device, except where a setting says
"on this device".

![The account menu](/img/screens/en/web-settings.png)

## Interface

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Language** | Bulgarian or English, everywhere at once |
| **Bulgarian names** | With Bulgarian on: show drugs, infusions, inhalational agents, fluids and premedication by their Bulgarian names. The record keeps the catalogue name; only what you see changes |
| **Theme** | Light or dark |
| **Display size** | Text, buttons and spacing larger or smaller — on this device only. Useful on a small phone or a large theatre screen |
| **Case screens** | How the preop, intraop and postop forms are laid out on the web: **Sidebar** keeps a panel beside the form with the patient, the sections still to complete and where the case is; without it, choose for each form **Sectioned** (tabs), **Scrollable** (one long page) or **Stacked**. On the phone: **Sections** (one at a time, swiped) or **Scroll** |
| **Timetable layout** | Which rows the intraoperative timeline shows, and in what order |
| **Vitals chart** | Whether the vitals chart above the timeline starts open |

## Units

Height, weight and temperature in the units you prefer. LOSPOR stores
canonical units (cm, kg, °C) and converts what you see and type.

## Automation

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Auto-fill vitals** | During a case, copies the last EtCO₂, SpO₂ and temperature into each empty 5-minute column as time passes. Copied values are marked "auto". It never fills the future, stops at the end of the case, and asks after 60 minutes without a typed entry whether the case is still running |
| **Auto-fill BP & HR** | Also carries forward blood pressure and heart rate (needs Auto-fill vitals) |
| **Backfill on reopen** | When a running case is reopened, offers to fill the empty columns of the last 30 minutes |
| **Favourite drugs / Favourite infusions** | Up to eight of each, offered first in the intraoperative menus |
| **Default monitoring** | The monitors ticked on a new case |

## Case reminders (phone)

Reminds you to chart vitals during a running case every 3, 5, 10 or 15
minutes; each entry resets the timer. In the browser app it works only while
LOSPOR and the case are open. It is a documentation aid, not a clinical alarm.

## Security & Access

Where you sign out of other devices, see when you last signed in, and ask for
the head-of-department role.

## Privacy & Data

| | |
|---|---|
| **Offline save queue** | Saves still waiting in this browser; you can discard them (cases already on the server are not affected) |
| **Export my data** | One archive with your account, every case you recorded, their history and your audit trail — what a request under GDPR Article 15 needs |
| **Delete my account** | Stops your access at once. Your clinical records stay with the department, with you named as their author |
