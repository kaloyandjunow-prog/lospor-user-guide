# Working on the phone

The phone app does everything the web does for a case, laid out for one hand
in theatre. How to put it on the phone is in
[Web and phone](../getting-started/web-and-phone.md); this page covers what is
different.

## The dashboard

![The dashboard on the phone](/img/screens/en/phone-dashboard.png)

- At the top: **Home**, search, **+** (new case) and **Settings**.
- The counts, **Unit indicators**, then the scopes and filters in a scrolling
  row.
- Each case card shows its status and the step to continue ("Open intraop",
  "Review case").
- **Hold a case** for its menu: **Procedure cancelled** (greyed out once
  anaesthesia has started), **Undo cancellation**, **Delete**, the operating
  theatre, **Hand over**, and on a finished case **Print case**.

![The menu of a held case](/img/screens/en/phone-menu.png)

## Entering numbers

- Age, height, weight and airway distances turn on a **wheel**: spin to the
  value, tap outside to keep it. **123** opens a keypad instead.
- Vitals use **−** and **+** (hold to repeat) and a slider. Tap the number to
  type it on the keypad.
- Commas and dots both work as the decimal mark.

## Searching

Diagnosis, procedure and drug fields show matches in a list right under the
field as you type. For a procedure, choose the group first, then
**Choose exact operation**.

## Taking photos

With **Allow AI assistance for this case** on:

- **Scan lab report** reads a printed lab report from the camera or gallery.
- **Scan monitor screen**, in the vitals entry, reads the values off the
  anaesthesia monitor.

The values are always shown for you to check before anything is saved. Keep
names and ID numbers out of the picture.

## In theatre

The intraoperative screen is described in
[The intraoperative record](./intraop.md#on-the-phone). In short: tabs across
the top, the timetable in 5-minute rows, tap a row to add, **Undo** after each
entry, **📊 Chart**, **↓ Now** and **End case** at the bottom.

## The case screen

![A finished case on the phone](/img/screens/en/phone-case.png)

Tapping a case opens its screen: the preoperative, intraoperative and
postoperative records as cards. From here:

- **Preop**, **Intraop**, **Postop** to edit a step, while the case is open;
- **Finalise case** to finish it, **Unfinalize** within 30 minutes after;
- **Print PDF**, which opens a PDF to print or send;
- **Share summary**;
- **Delete**, for a case created by mistake.

A finished or cancelled case opens read-only, and its timetable opens as the
chart rather than the live screen.

## Without a connection

Keep working. Entries are kept on the phone and sent when the connection
returns; the header shows **Saved** or that entries are waiting.

If the server refuses an entry (for example a stop before its start), it is
not lost: **Unsaved events** (open **Settings**, then **Settings** again) lists
every refused entry, so you can
re-enter it correctly. **Clear list** removes them from the phone.

## Settings on the phone

**Settings** holds your language, the light or dark theme, the form layout,
your drug and infusion favourites, auto-fill of vitals, and your account
(hospital, sign out). See [Your settings](../getting-started/settings.md).

Signing out with entries still waiting warns you first: they would be lost.

### Diagnostics

**Settings → Diagnostics** shows what the app is doing: edits waiting to be
sent, whether the server is reachable, and how fast the app responds. If
LOSPOR feels slow or something does not save, this is the screen to show
whoever supports you.
