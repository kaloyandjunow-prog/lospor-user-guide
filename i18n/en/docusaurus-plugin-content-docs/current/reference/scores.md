# Scores

The scores LOSPOR works out or asks for, and how it reads them. They support
your judgement; they never replace it.

A score worked out from questions shows how many of its questions were
actually answered: an unasked question is not counted as "no".

## Before surgery

| Score | What it is | How LOSPOR reads it |
|---|---|---|
| **ASA** | Physical status class, I–VI (E for emergency) | Required. LOSPOR **suggests** a class from the coded diagnoses, comorbidities and BMI, and names the reasons; you choose |
| **RCRI** | Revised Cardiac Risk Index: 6 factors (high-risk surgery, ischaemic heart disease, heart failure, cerebrovascular disease, insulin-treated diabetes, creatinine > 177 µmol/L) | 0 very low (0.4%), 1 low (1.0%), 2 moderate (2.4%), 3+ high (≥ 5.4%) major cardiac events |
| **Gupta (MICA)** | Myocardial infarction or cardiac arrest risk from age, functional status, ASA, creatinine and procedure type | A percentage; above 1% is elevated. Only when the hospital switches its questions on |
| **Apfel** | Postoperative nausea and vomiting: female, non-smoker, history of PONV or motion sickness, postoperative opioids | 0 low (~10%), 1–2 moderate (~20–40%), 3–4 high (~60–80%) |
| **STOP-BANG** | Obstructive sleep apnoea: snoring, tiredness, observed apnoea, high blood pressure, BMI > 35, age > 50, neck > 40 cm, male | 0–2 low, 3–4 intermediate, 5–8 high likelihood |
| **Mallampati** | Airway: class I–IV from the view of the pharynx | Recorded at the airway evaluation; part of what a case needs to finish (or "unable to obtain") |

### Children

| Score | What it is | How LOSPOR reads it |
|---|---|---|
| **POVOC** | Postoperative vomiting in children: surgery ≥ 30 min, age ≥ 3 years, strabismus surgery, personal or family history of vomiting after surgery | 0–4 factors: about 9%, 10%, 30%, 55%, 70% |
| **COLDS** | For a child with a current or recent upper respiratory infection: current symptoms, onset, lung disease, airway device, surgery — each 1, 2 or 5 points | A total from 5 to 25; the higher, the greater the risk of respiratory complications. Advisory |
| **Broselow** | Colour band by height (or weight) | Used for equipment sizes and doses |

## After surgery

| Score | What it is | How LOSPOR reads it |
|---|---|---|
| **Modified Aldrete** (adults) | Activity, respiration, circulation, consciousness, SpO₂, each 0–2 | 9–10 ready for discharge from recovery; 7–8 extended monitoring; below 7 not ready |
| **Steward** (children) | Consciousness, airway, movement, each 0–2 | 4 or more ready; below 4 not ready |
| **NRS** | Pain 0–10, as the patient says | Adults and children from 8 years |
| **FLACC** | Pain 0–10 from face, legs, activity, cry, consolability | Children from 28 days to under 8 years |
| **FPS-R** | Faces Pain Scale – Revised, 0–10: the child points to a face | Children from 28 days to under 8 years |
| **NIPS** | Neonatal Infant Pain Scale, 0–7 | Newborns under 28 days |
| **PAED** | Paediatric Anaesthesia Emergence Delirium, 0–20 | Below 10 none, 10–12 possible, above 12 likely emergence delirium |

Pain above 3 (the hospital may set another threshold) counts as moderate or
severe pain in the [unit indicators](../head-of-department/unit-indicators.md).

## Body measures

| | |
|---|---|
| **BMI** | Weight ÷ height² |
| **IBW** | Ideal body weight (Devine for adults; McLaren for children) |
| **ABW** | Adjusted body weight, IBW + 0.4 × (actual − IBW), when the actual weight is above IBW |
| **BSA** | Body surface area (Mosteller), for children |
