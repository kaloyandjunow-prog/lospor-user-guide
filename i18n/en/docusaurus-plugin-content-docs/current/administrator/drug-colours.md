# Drug colours

**Clinical setup → Drug colours** sets the colour of each drug on the
intraoperative chart and in its menus, on the web and on the phone.

![Drug colours](/img/screens/en/status-control_drug-colors.png)

- Drugs are listed by group (IV anaesthetics, sedatives, antiemetics, …).
  **Search a drug or a group** finds one.
- A drug takes its **group's colour** unless it has one of its own. **Change
  the group's colour** changes every drug in the group that has none.
- **Change** beside a drug gives it its own colour. Its bolus and its infusion
  share it.

No case stores a colour: every case, earlier ones included, is drawn in the
colours set here. Choose colours that stay distinct from each other in both
the light and the dark theme.
