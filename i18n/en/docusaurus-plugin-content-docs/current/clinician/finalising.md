# Reviewing and finishing a case

After the postoperative step the case opens as a **summary**: the whole
record as it will print. This is the moment to check it.

![The case summary](/img/screens/en/web-case-summary.png)

## The 30-minute review

Saving the postoperative step starts a **30-minute review**. The bar above the
summary reads **Case pending close — auto-closes in** and counts down.

While it runs you can:

- correct anything: **Edit: Preop · Intraop · Postop** opens that step;
- **Close Now** to finish the case at once.

When the 30 minutes are up, the case closes by itself, signed as yours. It
does not matter whether the page is still open.

A case that is not complete is never closed, by you or by the timer. If
something required is missing, **Close Now** shows how many things
(**Close Now (2)**) and lists them, each with a link to the field.

## Finished

A finished case is final: its record can no longer be changed. LOSPOR offers
to print it straight away (**Print case**, or **Not now**). See
[Printing](./printing.md).

### Changed your mind?

For 30 minutes after a case is finished, **Unfinalize** reopens it for
editing. After that it stays final.

## At the top of the summary

- The **case code** (for example `2026-0498`), which ties the printed record
  to LOSPOR.
- The **operating theatre**, when the hospital uses them.
- **Notes**: the case's notes for the team.

## Who can do what

Only the clinician the case belongs to can edit, close or unfinalize it. A
colleague who handed the case on, and others in the same hospital who may see
it, can read and print it.

## On the phone

![A finished case on the phone](/img/screens/en/phone-case.png)

The phone shows the same summary, with the same actions: edit a step, close,
print and share.
