# Assigning cases

A head of department distributes the day's cases with **Assign to**. Unlike a
member's **Hand over**, the colleague does not need to accept: the case moves
at once.

## Assigning a case

1. On the dashboard, find the case. The **Today** scope and the **operating
   theatre** filter help to see one list at a time.
2. Press **Assign to** in its row (on the phone: hold the case).
3. Choose the colleague. Heads of department are marked **HOD** in the list.
4. **Assign now**.

The case is now the colleague's: it appears on their dashboard and they can
edit it. You can still see and edit it, as you can every case of the
department.

## Assigning the operating theatre

When the hospital uses operating theatres, the **Theatre** button in the
case's row sets or changes the theatre, until anaesthesia starts. If a
colleague changed it while your menu was open, LOSPOR shows what they chose
and you choose again. See [The dashboard](../clinician/dashboard.md).

## Seeing who has what

- The **anaesthesiologist** filter on the dashboard shows one colleague's
  cases.
- **Ongoing cases** in the top bar lists the open cases.
- The **Handovers** scope lists cases waiting for a colleague to accept.

## What cannot be assigned

Finished and cancelled cases. A case can belong to only one clinician at a
time, and the case code may change when it moves: see
[Handing a case over](../clinician/handover.md#what-moves-and-what-does-not).
