# Indicator thresholds

**Clinical setup → Unit indicators** sets how the
[unit indicators](../head-of-department/unit-indicators.md) count events.

![Unit indicator settings](/img/screens/en/status-control_indicators.png)

## Hypotension, by age

For each age band, a reading is hypotensive below the **MAP** or the
**systolic** value set; leave one empty to not use it. The bundled values:

| Age band | Bundled |
|---|---|
| Newborn (under 28 days) | SBP < 60 |
| Infant (28 days to 1 year) | SBP < 70 |
| 1 to 5 years | SBP < 75 |
| 6 to 9 years | SBP < 85 |
| 10 to 17 years | SBP < 90 |
| 18 to 64 years | MAP < 65 |
| 65 years and over | MAP < 65 |

Time in hypotension is counted from the chart's readings, each standing for
its 5 minutes, up to 15 minutes until the next.

## Other thresholds

| Threshold | Bundled |
|---|---|
| Desaturation: SpO₂ below | 90 % |
| Hypothermia: temperature below | 36 °C |
| Recovery pain: score above (NRS, FLACC, FPS-R, NIPS) | 3 |
| Emergence delirium: PAED at least | 10 |

## Naming colleagues

**Show colleagues by name to the whole department** — off by default: each
clinician sees their own opioid figures by name and colleagues as letters.
On, everyone sees every colleague by name. This is monitoring of staff:
switch it on only with the department's agreement.

**Save the unit indicators settings** with a reason and your password. The
figures are worked out again with the new values.
