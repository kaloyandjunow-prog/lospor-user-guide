# The postoperative step

The postoperative step records how the patient left theatre and recovery:
the recovery vitals, a recovery score, pain and nausea, what was actually
done, and where the patient goes next.

![The postoperative form](/img/screens/en/web-postop.png)

It has four sections: **Recovery vitals**, **Recovery score**, **Pain &
PONV** and **Discharge**. A red dot in the side panel marks a section with
something required still missing.

## Recovery vitals

Systolic and diastolic blood pressure, heart rate, SpO₂ and temperature, with
**−** and **+**, a slider, or typed in. If a value could not be measured,
press **Unable to obtain** beside it instead of leaving it empty.

## Recovery score

Scored on leaving theatre. Each item is a row of buttons, 0 to 2.

- **Adults: Modified Aldrete Score** — activity, respiration, circulation
  (blood pressure against the preop value), consciousness, SpO₂. A total of 9
  or more is ready for discharge from recovery; below that, extended recovery
  monitoring.
- **Children: Steward recovery score** — consciousness, airway, movement. A
  total of 4 or more is ready.

For **SpO₂** and **circulation**, LOSPOR offers a suggestion worked out from
the recovery vitals you entered (for example "Suggests 2: systolic 118 against
130 mmHg before surgery"). **Use** takes it; nothing is filled in for you.

## Pain and nausea

- **Pain:** the scales offered depend on age. An adult, or a child of 8 or
  more, has **NRS** (0–10). A younger child has **FLACC** and **FPS-R** (the
  faces), a newborn **NIPS**. Fill in any of them, or none.
- **PONV** (postoperative nausea and vomiting): **Yes** or **No**. Left
  unanswered it reads "not asked", not "no".
- For a child there is also **Emergence delirium (PAED)**. See
  [Children](./children.md).

## Discharge

![Discharge from recovery](/img/screens/en/web-postop-discharge.png)

- **Procedure done:** filled in with the planned procedure. Change it if the
  operation was different, and choose the exact operation if you can. If the
  procedure was marked *not yet known* in the preop form, this is where you
  record what was done, and the case cannot be finished without it.
- **Dispatch to:** **Ward**, **PACU** or **ICU**. Required.
- **Instructions / handover notes:** anything the next team needs. Never names
  or ID numbers.

## Saving

**Save case & generate protocol** saves the step and opens
[the summary](./finalising.md), where you review the whole case before it is
finished.

## On the phone

![The postoperative step on the phone](/img/screens/en/phone-postop.png)

The same sections, one after the other. Vitals use the same − / + controls;
tap the number to type it.
